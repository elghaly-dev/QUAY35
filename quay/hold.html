QUAY · Hold

What QUAY will not build.

These come up often. Here is why the answer is no, and what would have to change.

Copy-trading

Copying trades automatically needs either your keys or a wallet we control, and we will not hold either.

Auto-panic sell

A button that sells for you is signing authority, and the day it fires wrongly the loss is ours to argue about and yours to carry.

Telegram escrow

Holding someone else's funds between two strangers makes us a custodian in a dispute we have no way to adjudicate.

These stay on hold until QUAY Land has three paying operators. Not because the risk changes — it does not — but because we will not learn custody on someone else's money.
