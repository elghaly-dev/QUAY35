How it works

Everything we read is already public on chain or in a block explorer.

  1. You send public dataSignatures, a mint address, the feed to watch, or the text for your cards. Never a key or an RPC URL with a token in it.
  2. We read itAgainst what actually landed on chain. If something cannot be read from public data, we say UNKNOWN instead of guessing.
  3. You get a file, a card or a pingBy email. Nothing of ours touches your config, your wallet or your transactions.
