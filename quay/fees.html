QUAY · Fee review

Priority-fee and tip configuration review

We read your public transaction signatures and compare your current priority-fee and tip settings against what those transactions actually paid and landed at.

You receive one written page within 48 hours of a complete brief. This is a recommendation only — we do not change your config, your wallet, or your transactions.

What you send

All of this is public data. Send nothing that is not already visible on chain or in a block explorer.

  • Up to 200 transaction signatures — signatures only. Never a private key, never a seed phrase, never an RPC URL with a token in it.
  • Your current priority-fee setting
  • Your current tip setting
  • Which venue or aggregator you route through

We will not ask for, display, or accept an API key, an RPC URL containing a token, or a private key.

What you get back

One page, delivered within 48 hours of a complete brief:

  • The percentile your current setting actually lands at
  • The percentile to target, with the reasoning shown
  • When to raise, and the specific condition that should trigger it
  • Where you are overpaying, with the wasted amount per landed tx
  • What we could not determine from the signatures, named explicitly

If an input cannot be read from a signature, the report says UNKNOWN — not a guess.

What we cannot tell you from signatures alone

Signatures show what was submitted and what landed on chain. They do not show everything about your operation. At minimum, we cannot see:

  • Your fills — whether a landed transaction achieved the trade outcome you wanted
  • Your strategy — intent, sizing logic, or timing rules behind each send
  • Your RPC latency — how long your node or provider took to propagate the transaction
  • Why a specific transaction failed if the failure is not recorded on chain (simulation rejections, dropped packets, client-side aborts)

Anything that cannot be derived from the signatures you send is marked UNKNOWN in the report. We do not infer or fill gaps.
